# Open business and operational decisions

1. Launch jurisdictions and the responsible legal entity in each market.
2. Seller of record, merchant of record, importer/exporter, tax liability, returns, warranty, and support ownership by route.
3. Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction eligibility and merchant onboarding for the
   implemented Stripe Checkout and Coinbase Business Checkout adapters, plus
   the separate marketplace settlement model.
4. Tax, restricted-party, identity-verification, and address-validation providers.
5. Data residency, retention periods, legal-hold policy, and disaster-recovery region.
6. C2C buyer-protection terms, prohibited products, payout hold, and dispute appeals.
7. B2B credit underwriting and collections ownership.
8. Required carrier/warehouse production integrations and contracted SLAs.
9. Production secrets manager and observability vendor.
10. Whether the single-host deployment is allowed for any production market.
11. Production SMTP, object malware-scanning, and optional GPS providers.
12. Whether a unified quote/payment/travel/exception lifecycle projection is
    required in addition to the current freight milestone timeline.
13. Licensed UN/LOCODE and other international reference-data sources,
    refresh frequency, and reconciliation owner.
14. GS1 company-prefix ownership and which party allocates SSCC, GINC, GSIN,
    and GLN identifiers.
15. Licensed cargo insurer/broker by tenant entity and route, supported
    Institute Cargo Clauses/editions, sanctions rules, premium tax, claims SLA,
    surveyor, reserve, and subrogation process.
16. Customs brokers, national single-window connectors, WCO Data Model
    profiles, commodity-classification provider, and importer/exporter of
    record by country.
17. Postal operator/designated-operator relationships, IMPC and S10 allocation,
    UPU EDI messages, transport handover agreements, remuneration, terminal
    dues, and return-to-sender policy.
18. Dangerous-goods content/provider and trained acceptance ownership for
    ADR/RID/IMDG/IATA DGR routes.
19. DCSA, IATA ONE Record, rail, carrier schedule, eBL/eCMR signature, GPS/IoT,
    and EPCIS adapter priorities.
