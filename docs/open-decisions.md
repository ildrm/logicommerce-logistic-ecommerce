1. Launch jurisdictions and the responsible legal entity in each market.
2. Seller of record, merchant of record, importer/exporter, tax liability, returns, warranty, and support ownership by route.
3. Licensed payment provider and marketplace settlement model.
4. Tax, restricted-party, identity-verification, and address-validation providers.
5. Data residency, retention periods, legal-hold policy, and disaster-recovery region.
6. C2C buyer-protection terms, prohibited products, payout hold, and dispute appeals.
7. B2B credit underwriting and collections ownership.
8. Required carrier/warehouse production integrations and contracted SLAs.
9. Production secrets manager and observability vendor.
10. Whether the single-host deployment is allowed for any production market.
