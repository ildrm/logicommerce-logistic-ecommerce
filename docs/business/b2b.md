# B2B boundary

B2B adds company hierarchy, roles, budgets, approvals, contract pricing, quotes,
purchase orders, payment terms, credit, invoices, partial delivery, and EDI/API entry.

Approved business freight customers may use configured prepayment, deposit, or
net terms subject to the account credit limit. New B2B and freight invoices use
the canonical invoice/schedule model; legacy B2B invoice rows remain historical
compatibility data.
